Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Good morning and welcome to the community arts centre booking office.
  2. Hello I would like to ask about the weekend music workshop.
  3. Certainly we organise guitar and piano sessions every Saturday morning.
  4. I am interested in the guitar class which takes place in room fifteen.
  5. Actually we changed the venue to room twelve because it is much larger.
  6. That is good to know since many students joined this month.
  7. Yes the fee is normally fifty pounds but students pay forty pounds.
  8. Do I need to bring my own acoustic guitar for the lesson?
  9. You must bring your own instrument although we supply extra music stands.
  10. Should I also bring a notebook to write down some chords?
  11. A notebook is helpful and please bring a pencil for your sheet music.
  12. Thank you for all your helpful guidance today.
