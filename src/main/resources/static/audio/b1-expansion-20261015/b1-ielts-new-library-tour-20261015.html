Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome everyone to our central library and thank you for joining this morning tour.
  2. My name is Sarah and I will show you our facilities and borrowing process today.
  3. We have three main study areas spread across the building for different needs.
  4. The ground floor contains our large computer lab and a quiet reading room.
  5. Students can use twenty desktop computers here without making any prior booking.
  6. Please remember that mobile phones must remain silent inside this reading zone.
  7. Moving up to the first floor you will find our fiction and history books.
  8. Group study tables are also available there if you need to collaborate.
  9. The top floor is our dedicated silent zone for serious academic research work.
  10. You can borrow up to six physical books at any one time.
  11. Standard loan periods last for three weeks before items must be renewed.
  12. Digital textbooks and journals are accessible online through your student account password.
  13. If you lose a library item please report the issue immediately at reception.
  14. Our friendly staff members are always happy to help you find resources.
  15. Thank you for listening and I hope you enjoy studying in our library.
