Đáp án và giải thích
  1. B — Người mua hàng đang tìm một chiếc áo len màu xanh dương.
    “cannot find this blue sweater” · Đoạn 1
  2. A — Hàng mới sẽ đến vào buổi sáng ngày mai.
    “arriving with our morning delivery truck tomorrow” · Đoạn 4
  3. B — Nhân viên sẽ giữ món đồ ở quầy dịch vụ khách hàng.
    “hold it at the customer service desk” · Đoạn 6
  4. B — Người gọi thứ hai muốn xác nhận thời gian nhận đồ đặt trước.
    “confirm when my ordered office furniture will be ready” · Đoạn 7
  5. B — Nhân viên cửa hàng vừa mới lắp ráp xong chiếc bàn.
    “staff finished assembling it a moment ago” · Đoạn 10
  6. C — Cửa hàng mở cửa đến 8 giờ tối nay.
    “We are open until eight o clock tonight” · Đoạn 12
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Excuse me because I cannot find this blue sweater in a medium size on the display rack.
  2. Let me check our storage inventory on this computer system right now to see if we have more.
  3. Thank you because my sister really wants that specific color for her birthday next week.
  4. We actually have two medium sweaters arriving with our morning delivery truck tomorrow.
  5. That is wonderful news so can you put one aside for me to purchase tomorrow afternoon?
  6. I will hold it at the customer service desk under your name if you provide it.
  7. Hello I am calling to confirm when my ordered office furniture will be ready for store collection.
  8. Good afternoon and please give me your receipt number so I can look up your order.
  9. My receipt number is four five nine two and I paid for the desk last Friday.
  10. Your large wooden desk arrived this morning and our staff finished assembling it a moment ago.
  11. Can I pick it up this evening after I finish working at my office?
  12. We are open until eight o clock tonight so you can collect it anytime before closing.
