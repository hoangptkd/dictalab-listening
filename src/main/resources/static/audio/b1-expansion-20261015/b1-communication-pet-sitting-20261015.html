Đáp án và giải thích
  1. A — Speaker A asks if Sarah can feed the cat while away.
    “I was wondering if you could feed my cat” · Đoạn 1
  2. B — The food is stored inside the kitchen cabinet next to the refrigerator.
    “His food is kept inside the kitchen cabinet” · Đoạn 5
  3. C — Speaker B promises to keep the water bowl filled with fresh water.
    “I will also make sure his water bowl is always filled” · Đoạn 8
  4. B — Speaker B states she will visit twice a day.
    “I can drop by twice a day” · Đoạn 10
  5. A — The cat loves to chase a small red ball.
    “He loves chasing a small red ball” · Đoạn 13
  6. B — Speaker B says she will grab the house key later today.
    “I will grab the key later today” · Đoạn 16
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Hello Sarah because I am going away this weekend I was wondering if you could feed my cat.
  2. I would be very happy to help you out while you are away from home.
  3. That is wonderful news because I always worry about leaving him alone for two whole days.
  4. You do not need to worry at all when you have a good neighbour to assist you.
  5. His food is kept inside the kitchen cabinet next to the large refrigerator.
  6. Should I give him one small bowl in the morning and another one at night?
  7. Yes exactly because he tends to eat everything quickly if I leave too much out.
  8. I will also make sure his water bowl is always filled with fresh cold water.
  9. Thank you so much because he can be quite picky about his drinking water.
  10. I can drop by twice a day once in the morning and once after work.
  11. That schedule sounds perfect although he might sleep through most of your visits.
  12. Does he like playing with any particular toys when he is awake?
  13. He loves chasing a small red ball if you have a few minutes to spare.
  14. I will definitely spend some time playing with him after he finishes eating.
  15. I left the spare house key under the blue flower pot by the front door.
  16. I will grab the key later today so I can check everything before you leave.
