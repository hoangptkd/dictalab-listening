Đáp án và giải thích
  1. B — Speaker A explicitly mentions that their presentation is about urban transport.
    “our upcoming presentation on urban transport yet” · Đoạn 1
  2. A — Sarah states that her first suggestion was comparing bicycle sharing schemes in three capitals.
    “comparing bicycle sharing schemes in three major European capitals” · Đoạn 3
  3. B — They discard the European capitals idea because collecting financial data from three cities would take too much time before their deadline.
    “gathering accurate financial data from three different cities might take longer” · Đoạn 5
  4. B — Speaker A suggests shifting the focus to their local city transport network, which they agree to do.
    “focus exclusively on our local city transport network” · Đoạn 7
  5. A — Speaker A asks Mark to design the questionnaire after she prepares the introduction slides.
    “while you design the questionnaire” · Đoạn 11
  6. B — Speaker A suggests meeting on Thursday afternoon to rehearse their speaking parts.
    “meet again on Thursday afternoon” · Đoạn 13
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Hello Mark, have you thought about our upcoming presentation on urban transport yet?
  2. Hi Sarah, I looked at a few interesting topics although I found most of them too complicated.
  3. My initial suggestion was comparing bicycle sharing schemes in three major European capitals.
  4. That sounds brilliant because bicycles are very popular when discussing modern green cities.
  5. However, gathering accurate financial data from three different cities might take longer than we expect.
  6. You are completely right if we consider our strict deadline next week.
  7. What if we focus exclusively on our local city transport network instead?
  8. That would be much easier because we can easily interview local commuters.
  9. We should also include a short survey about recent bus punctuality improvements.
  10. Students love real statistics when they watch presentations in our seminar group.
  11. Shall I prepare the introduction slides while you design the questionnaire?
  12. That division of labour works perfectly for me today.
  13. Let us meet again on Thursday afternoon to practise our speaking parts together.
  14. I will bring the printed handouts then so we are fully prepared.
