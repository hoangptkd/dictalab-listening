Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome today to our urban geography lecture about small green spaces in modern cities.
  2. Many planners overlook tiny empty lots because they prefer massive parks for recreation.
  3. However pocket parks offer incredible benefits if local residents need quiet places to relax.
  4. Urban developers usually plant native trees because these plants require very little extra water.
  5. Researchers frequently measure air temperature because shaded areas reduce summer heat significantly.
  6. If you visit a garden today you might notice comfortable wooden benches for visitors.
  7. Another common feature is colorful flowers which attract local bees and butterflies.
  8. When scientists study visitor habits they often conduct brief interviews near the entrance.
  9. Observers should always record the exact time because visitor numbers change throughout the day.
  10. Morning hours typically attract elderly neighbors who enjoy reading under peaceful morning shadows.
  11. By contrast afternoon periods bring energetic children after finishing their daily school classes.
  12. Supposing an observer wanted to count users carefully a handheld clicker helps enormously.
  13. Please remember that safety matters most so you should always wear comfortable walking shoes.
  14. Your final written report must include a detailed map highlighting every major seating area.
