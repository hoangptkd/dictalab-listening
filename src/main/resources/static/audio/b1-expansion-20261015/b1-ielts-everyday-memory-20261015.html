Đáp án và giải thích
  1. hook — Người nói đề xuất đặt chìa khóa lên một cái móc cố định gần cửa.
    “designated hook” · Đoạn 5
  2. kitchen — Ví dụ hình dung đồ vật trên bàn bếp thay vì viết giấy.
    “kitchen table” · Đoạn 8
  3. auditory — Nhắc nhở bằng âm thanh giúp gián đoạn công việc hiện tại.
    “Auditory reminders” · Đoạn 13
  4. colored — Giấy nhớ có màu sắc giúp thu hút sự chú ý.
    “colored stickers” · Đoạn 14
  5. monitor — Dán giấy nhắc việc lên màn hình máy tính.
    “computer monitor” · Đoạn 15

    “tomorrow morning” · Đoạn 17
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome everyone to this short introductory lecture about practical memory techniques for everyday tasks.
  2. Many people struggle to recall simple things although they have otherwise sharp minds.
  3. We will explore several common methods today through hypothetical examples rather than strict science.
  4. Suppose you constantly forget where you put your keys when you arrive home.
  5. A very simple habit is establishing a designated hook near the front door.
  6. Whenever you enter the house you place them there without exception.
  7. Another common issue is remembering items you need to buy at the grocery store.
  8. Instead of writing a long list on paper you might visualize your kitchen table.
  9. You then imagine placing a giant loaf of bread right on top of it.
  10. Such unusual mental pictures make the items much harder to forget later.
  11. People also forget important appointments because they rely entirely on their memory.
  12. If you set an alarm on your phone you remove the stress completely.
  13. Auditory reminders are extremely effective because they interrupt whatever you are doing.
  14. Some individuals prefer writing notes on small colored stickers.
  15. You can attach these sticky notes to your computer monitor before you sleep.
  16. When morning arrives you see the bright message immediately upon waking up.
  17. Try practicing just one of these methods starting tomorrow morning.
  18. Thank you for your attention and good luck with your daily organization.
