Nghe hai đoạn hội thoại ngắn tại nơi làm việc và trả lời sáu câu hỏi trắc nghiệm bên dưới.

Đáp án và giải thích
  1. A — Người nói nhắc đến buổi thuyết trình cho khách hàng vào thứ Ba tới.
    “We need to select a suitable demonstration room for the upcoming client presentation next Tuesday.” · Đoạn 1
  2. B — Phòng này quá chật chội cho mười hai người tham dự.
    “That room might be too cramped because we are expecting twelve people to attend the session.” · Đoạn 3
  3. B — Người thứ hai đề xuất chuyển sang phòng hội trường chính.
    “We could easily move the workshop to the main auditorium if they approve the request.” · Đoạn 4
  4. B — Cuộc gọi nhằm sắp xếp việc giao hàng mẫu sản phẩm.
    “Good morning because I am calling to arrange the delivery of the product samples we ordered last week.” · Đoạn 9
  5. A — Lô hàng đang ở kho trung tâm.
    “Our logistics department received your shipment yesterday afternoon at the central warehouse.” · Đoạn 10
  6. B — Phải trả thêm phí để tài xế mang đồ lên lầu.
    “The delivery driver usually drops everything off downstairs unless you pay an extra fee.” · Đoạn 12
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. We need to select a suitable demonstration room for the upcoming client presentation next Tuesday.
  2. I checked the schedule and only the small conference suite on the third floor is available.
  3. That room might be too cramped because we are expecting twelve people to attend the session.
  4. We could easily move the workshop to the main auditorium if they approve the request.
  5. The auditorium is already booked for a morning seminar by the marketing team.
  6. Let us ask management to swap our booking since our presentation is much more critical.
  7. That sounds like a risky plan which might cause unnecessary conflicts with colleagues.
  8. I will call the facilities manager right now to see if another medium room was recently cancelled.
  9. Good morning because I am calling to arrange the delivery of the product samples we ordered last week.
  10. Our logistics department received your shipment yesterday afternoon at the central warehouse.
  11. Could you deliver those heavy boxes directly to our office on the fourth floor instead?
  12. The delivery driver usually drops everything off downstairs unless you pay an extra fee.
  13. We can definitely cover the additional cost if your team brings them up before noon.
  14. I will inform the driver immediately so they arrive at your desk by eleven o clock.
