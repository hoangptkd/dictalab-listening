Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Good morning I would like to renew my borrowed books because I am still reading them.
  2. Good morning please let me check your library account on my computer screen right now.
  3. Thank you my library card number is printed clearly on the back of the plastic.
  4. I see your account and notice that two novels are already overdue by three days.
  5. Oh I am sorry about that delay because I was quite busy studying for exams.
  6. Do not worry since you can renew them online or right here at the desk.
  7. Can I renew them for another two weeks even though another reader wants them?
  8. Unfortunately if another member places a reservation you cannot extend the borrowing period anymore.
  9. Nobody has reserved these specific books yet so can I keep them longer today?
  10. Yes because there are no pending requests I can easily extend both items for you now.
  11. That is wonderful news and I will definitely finish reading them before next week arrives.
  12. Remember that if you forget next time you might receive a small daily fine charge.
  13. I understand completely and I will set a reminder on my smartphone calendar today.
  14. Is there anything else I can help you with regarding your membership privileges today?
  15. No everything is perfectly clear now and I appreciate your friendly assistance this morning.
  16. You are very welcome and have a wonderful time reading your interesting library books.
