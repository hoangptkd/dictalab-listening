    “portable generator” · Đoạn 5

    “community library” · Đoạn 6
  5. main — Tình nguyện viên tập trung gần đài phun nước chính ('main fountain').
    “main fountain” · Đoạn 8

Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome everyone to our planning meeting for the upcoming summer market.
  2. This year the event will take place at the central park on the first Saturday of July.
  3. Local vendors can rent a standard stall for forty pounds for the entire day.
  4. Each stall includes one large table and two folding chairs under a waterproof canopy.
  5. If you plan to sell hot food items you must bring your own portable generator.
  6. Parking for stall holders is available behind the community library until eight in the morning.
  7. Public visitors should use the underground station because the main street will be closed to cars.
  8. Volunteers must gather near the main fountain at nine to collect their name badges.
  9. Free drinking water will be provided inside the main information tent all afternoon.
  10. Please remember to submit your completed insurance forms before Friday afternoon.
  11. We will also have a special recycling station located next to the entrance gates.
  12. All participants must ensure their rubbish is properly sorted before leaving the grounds.
  13. Any extra tables can be requested at the office before the end of this week.
  14. Thank you all for your continuous support and dedication to making this market successful.
