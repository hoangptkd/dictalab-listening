Đáp án và giải thích
  1. nine — Người nói cho biết cổng chính mở cửa vào lúc chín giờ sáng (nine).
    “nine in the morning” · Đoạn 3
  2. south — Người tham dự được hướng dẫn đỗ xe ở bãi đỗ xe phía nam (south).
    “south car park” · Đoạn 6
  3. ten — Vé người lớn mua trực tuyến có giá mười bảng (ten).
    “ten pounds each” · Đoạn 8
  4. desk — Tiền mặt cần được nộp tại bàn tài chính (desk) bên trong lều.
    “finance desk” · Đoạn 9
  5. three — Buổi biểu diễn múa đã được chuyển sang buổi chiều bắt đầu lúc ba giờ (three).
    “starting at three” · Đoạn 13
  6. yellow — Tình nguyện viên cần mặc áo lê-ghê màu vàng (yellow) của họ.
    “yellow vests” · Đoạn 16
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome everyone to our final planning meeting for the upcoming neighbourhood spring festival.
  2. Before we open the gates on Saturday morning, I need to share some essential practical details with you all.
  3. Let us start with the main entrance gate which opens at nine in the morning for early visitors.
  4. Parking spaces are very limited this year because the west field is currently being repaired by the council.
  5. Therefore, we strongly advise every attendee to use public buses or arrive on bicycles if possible.
  6. If you drive a vehicle, you must leave it in the south car park near the library.
  7. Moving on to ticket prices, general admission remains completely free for children and senior citizens.
  8. Adult tickets cost ten pounds each when purchased online or twelve pounds at the entrance.
  9. Please remember that all cash payments should be handed to the main finance desk inside the tent.
  10. Regarding our schedule, there is one important programme change you should note down right now.
  11. The traditional dancing show was originally scheduled for noon on the central wooden stage.
  12. However, the dance troupe leader called us this morning to report a sudden illness among performers.
  13. Consequently, we have moved the dancing performance to the afternoon slot starting at three.
  14. In its original morning slot, we will instead host a local acoustic guitar concert for visitors.
  15. Food stalls will operate throughout the day offering hot meals and refreshing cold drinks.
  16. Volunteers should report to the information booth wearing their bright yellow vests by eight.
