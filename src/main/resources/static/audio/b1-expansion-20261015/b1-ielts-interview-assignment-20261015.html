  2. B — Họ chỉ hỏi về kế hoạch mở rộng nếu thời gian cho phép khi người quản lý trả lời xong các câu hỏi chính.

Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. We need to prepare our questions for the upcoming college interview project.
  2. I already drafted five open questions about local business management.
  3. Those sound great although we should probably add something about digital marketing too.
  4. That is a smart idea because every company uses social media nowadays.
  5. Should we also ask about their future expansion plans if time permits?
  6. Only if the manager finishes answering the core questions quickly enough.
  7. Who should we contact first to schedule this recorded interview?
  8. My uncle runs a small bookstore downtown so he might agree to help us.
  9. That location is convenient when we want to carry our heavy recording equipment.
  10. Let us call him tomorrow morning before someone else books his schedule.
  11. Do we need to bring extra microphones or will your smartphone recorder suffice?
  12. We definitely need the external microphone because ambient noise can ruin the audio.
  13. I will charge both batteries tonight so we avoid any technical failures.
  14. Perfect and I will print out the consent forms for him to sign.
  15. Everything is organized now so we can definitely finish this assignment successfully.
  16. I agree completely with your plan so let us meet outside the library tomorrow.
