Đáp án và giải thích
  1. A — Speaker A states that twelve more people registered yesterday, which requires updating the guest count.
    “twelve more people registered yesterday” · Đoạn 1
  2. B — Speaker B suggests asking the caterer for extra vegetarian choices to accommodate the new guests.
    “ask if they can provide extra vegetarian options” · Đoạn 4
  3. A — Speaker A mentions checking the dietary requirements list while speaker B speaks on the phone.
    “I will check the updated dietary requirements list” · Đoạn 5
  4. B — Sarah explains that one box is missing the essential metal screws needed for assembly.
    “one box is missing the necessary metal screws” · Đoạn 9
  5. A — The supplier promised to send a person with the replacement parts during the afternoon.
    “promised to send someone with the replacement parts this afternoon” · Đoạn 11
  6. C — Speaker A says they will inform reception to expect the courier around two o'clock.
    “inform the reception staff to expect the courier” · Đoạn 14
Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Hi Mark, we need to update the guest count for our annual conference next week because twelve more people registered yesterday.
  2. That is wonderful news for our attendance rate, but it means we must change our food orders quickly.
  3. Should we call the catering company right now before they buy the ingredients for this weekend?
  4. Yes, let us ring them immediately and also ask if they can provide extra vegetarian options.
  5. I will check the updated dietary requirements list while you talk to the manager on the phone.
  6. Great, I will also ask them to send a revised invoice by this afternoon.
  7. Perfect, let us meet again at noon to confirm everything is settled.
  8. Good morning Sarah, did you receive the final delivery of new office chairs that we ordered last Tuesday?
  9. They arrived at the reception desk this morning, but unfortunately one box is missing the necessary metal screws.
  10. That is very frustrating since the team needs to assemble those desks before the clients arrive tomorrow.
  11. I already contacted the supplier, and they promised to send someone with the replacement parts this afternoon.
  12. Will they charge us an extra delivery fee for fixing their mistake so quickly?
  13. No, because it was their error, they agreed to provide the missing parts completely free of charge.
  14. That is a relief, so I will inform the reception staff to expect the courier around two o'clock.
