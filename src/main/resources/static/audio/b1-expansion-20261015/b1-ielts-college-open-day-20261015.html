Đáp án và giải thích
  1. science building — Người nói thông báo buổi nói chuyện chính đã được chuyển đến tòa nhà khoa học.
    “we have moved that session to the science building” · Đoạn 4
  2. library — Các tour tham quan có hướng dẫn khởi hành từ thư viện.
    “guided walking tours leaving every thirty minutes from the library” · Đoạn 6
  3. central courtyard — Hội chợ khoa ngành được tổ chức ở sân trung tâm.

  4. pavilion — Bữa trưa được phục vụ tại nhà triển lãm (pavilion) do quán ăn tự phục vụ đóng cửa.

  5. security — Đồ thất lạc cần được báo cáo cho bộ phận bảo vệ.
    “report the missing item to security” · Đoạn 12
  6. feedback form — Khách tham quan được nhắc nhở hoàn thành mẫu phản hồi trước khi ra về.

Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Welcome everyone to our annual college open day and thank you for joining us this morning.
  2. Before you start exploring the campus, I need to share some important updates regarding our schedule.
  3. Our main welcome talk was originally scheduled for the large auditorium near the southern entrance.
  4. However because of unexpected maintenance work, we have moved that session to the science building.
  5. You will find clear directional signs pointing towards the new location as soon as you step outside.
  6. Regarding today's activities, you can join guided walking tours leaving every thirty minutes from the library.
  7. If you prefer exploring independently, pick up a detailed campus map from the main reception desk.
  8. Our department fair is hosted in the central courtyard where all academic advisors are waiting.
  9. You can ask questions about course requirements, tuition fees and student accommodation options directly.
  10. Please note that the cafeteria is closed for renovation today so lunch is served in the pavilion.
  11. Free sandwiches and hot beverages will be provided for all registered visitors starting at noon.
  12. If you lose any personal belongings during your visit, report the missing item to security.
  13. Our lost property office is conveniently located right beside the main entrance gates.
  14. Before you leave this afternoon, remember to complete our feedback form to win a prize.
  15. Thank you again for coming and I hope you thoroughly enjoy discovering our vibrant college community.
