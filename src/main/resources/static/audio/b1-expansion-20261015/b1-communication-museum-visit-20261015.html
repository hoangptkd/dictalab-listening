  3. C — Họ chọn đi xe buýt tốc hành vì trạm dừng ngay bên ngoài tòa nhà căn hộ của Sarah.

  4. A — Nếu đặt vé qua trang web của bảo tàng hôm nay, họ sẽ nhận được mức giảm giá mười phần trăm.
    “we get a ten percent discount” · Đoạn 11

    “Let us buy the combined ticket so we do not miss any of the amazing galleries” · Đoạn 15

Transcript và nghe từng đoạn
  1. Hi Mark we really need to plan our trip to the modern art museum this weekend.
  2. Hello Sarah I have been looking at the schedule and we should decide how to travel there.
  3. Although driving sounds comfortable the parking fees near the gallery are extremely expensive.
  4. That is very true because city centre parking usually costs twenty pounds for the whole day.
  5. We could take the underground train if we want to avoid all that terrible traffic.
  6. The metro is fast but it is always crowded when people travel on Saturday mornings.
  7. What about catching the local express bus since the stop is right outside my apartment building?
  8. Taking the bus sounds brilliant because we can enjoy the view and save some money.
  9. Now we also need to figure out the best way to purchase our entrance tickets.
  10. Buying them online beforehand is much cheaper than paying at the main entrance desk.
  11. If we book through the museum website today we get a ten percent discount.
  12. That is wonderful news because every little saving helps us buy extra coffee later.
  13. Should we visit the special sculpture exhibition or just look at the permanent collections?
  14. The sculpture exhibition looks fascinating although it requires an extra ticket upgrade.
  15. Let us buy the combined ticket so we do not miss any of the amazing galleries.
  16. I agree completely with your idea for the tickets.
  17. I will book the tickets on my laptop right now while you check the bus timetable.
  18. Perfect plan because everything will be ready for our wonderful Saturday adventure.
