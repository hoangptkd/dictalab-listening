# DictaLab — 9 bài nghe mẫu

Transcript gốc, câu hỏi và đáp án. IELTS/TOEIC là nhãn mục tiêu luyện, chưa hiệu chuẩn điểm thi.


## IELTS 4.0 — Đăng ký lớp làm gốm

Part 1 · Form completion

Bài luyện trích đoạn theo Part 1, gồm 6 câu điền thông tin. Viết ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER cho mỗi câu. Xem câu hỏi trước, nghe một lần rồi kiểm tra đáp án. Mục tiêu 4.0 là nhãn luyện tập, chưa hiệu chuẩn.

### Transcript

1. **A** (0.00–3.92s): Good morning, I'm calling about the pottery course at your community centre.
2. **B** (4.12–7.96s): Of course, are you interested in the weekday class or the weekend class?
3. **A** (8.16–11.44s): The weekend class, please, because I work every weekday.
4. **B** (11.64–15.80s): That class meets on Saturday, and it is suitable for complete beginners.
5. **A** (16.00–19.20s): Excellent, I have never made anything from clay before.
6. **B** (19.40–22.68s): The class starts at ten thirty and finishes at twelve.
7. **A** (22.88–26.00s): I thought the price was thirty pounds for each session.
8. **B** (26.20–29.88s): That was last year's price, but it is forty-two pounds now.
9. **A** (30.08–33.28s): Does that include everything, or do I need to buy some tools?
10. **B** (33.48–37.08s): The tools and clay are included, but please bring an apron.
11. **A** (37.28–40.32s): I can do that, and who will be teaching the course?
12. **B** (40.52–45.32s): Your teacher is Maya, spelled M, A, Y, A.
13. **A** (45.52–48.72s): Do I pay online before I come, or can I pay there?
14. **B** (48.92–53.00s): Please pay at reception when you arrive, using cash or a card.
15. **A** (53.20–56.40s): Great, is the class in the main hall near the entrance?
16. **B** (56.60–59.96s): No, it is in the studio, which is behind the main hall.
17. **A** (60.16–63.12s): Thank you, I will arrive a little early on Saturday.
18. **B** (63.32–66.76s): Perfect, then you will have time to pay and find your room.

### Câu hỏi

1. Class day: ______

2. Starting time: ______

3. Current session fee: £______

4. Item to bring: an ______

5. Teacher's first name: ______

6. Classroom: the ______

### Đáp án

1. **Saturday** — Ngày cuối cùng được xác nhận là Saturday. (Đoạn 4: “meets on Saturday”)
2. **10:30** — 12 giờ là giờ kết thúc, không phải giờ bắt đầu. (Đoạn 6: “starts at ten thirty”)
3. **42** — 30 là giá cũ; người nói sửa thành 42. (Đoạn 8: “forty-two pounds now”)
4. **apron** — Tools và clay đã được cung cấp. (Đoạn 10: “bring an apron”)
5. **Maya** — Tên được đánh vần ngay sau đó. (Đoạn 12: “Your teacher is Maya”)
6. **studio** — Main hall là thông tin bị bác bỏ. (Đoạn 16: “in the studio”)

## IELTS 5.0 — Thảo luận dự án không gian học tập

Part 3 · Multiple choice + note completion

Trích đoạn Part 3 với hai sinh viên. Câu 1–5 chọn A, B hoặc C. Câu 6 viết ONE WORD ONLY. Đọc câu hỏi trước và nghe một lần. Đây là bài luyện mục tiêu 5.0, không phải đề đo band.

### Transcript

1. **A** (0.00–3.92s): Have you finished reviewing the feedback for our campus study-space project?
2. **B** (4.12–7.80s): Mostly, but we should compare the busy area with the quiet room.
3. **A** (8.00–13.20s): I agree, because counting empty seats alone would not explain students' preferences.
4. **B** (13.40–17.80s): We planned an online survey, but the tutor thought the replies might be too brief.
5. **A** (18.00–22.72s): Short interviews should give us more detail about why people choose a particular area.
6. **B** (22.92–26.44s): Exactly, and we can still count how many seats are occupied.
7. **A** (26.64–30.24s): Should we collect our information just before the examinations begin?
8. **B** (30.44–35.00s): I'd rather use a normal teaching week, since exam pressure could change people's habits.
9. **A** (35.20–39.60s): That makes sense, so let's start next Monday and visit at different times.
10. **B** (39.80–44.28s): I can cover the mornings, but my laboratory classes take up most afternoons.
11. **A** (44.48–48.16s): Then I'll handle the afternoons and put our numbers into a spreadsheet.
12. **B** (48.36–51.96s): I'll write the interview questions, although the tutor should check them first.
13. **A** (52.16–55.36s): What about recommending some larger tables for group work?
14. **B** (55.56–59.16s): Maybe, but we should wait until we know what students actually need.
15. **A** (59.36–63.52s): You're right, because a bigger table might simply make the quiet room noisier.
16. **B** (63.72–67.80s): We also need to separate our observations from our personal opinions.
17. **A** (68.00–71.52s): I'll create two sections in our notes so that we don't mix them up.
18. **B** (71.72–74.76s): Let's send the interview questions to the tutor this Friday.
19. **A** (74.96–78.72s): Good plan, that gives us time to make any changes before Monday.

### Câu hỏi

1. Why do the students reject the online survey?
   - A. It would cost too much.
   - B. Its answers may lack detail.
   - C. Students cannot access it.

2. When will they collect information?
   - A. During a normal teaching week.
   - B. Immediately before examinations.
   - C. During the holidays.

3. Which task will speaker A do?
   - A. Write the interview questions.
   - B. Visit only in the mornings.
   - C. Record the numbers in a spreadsheet.

4. What do they decide about larger tables?
   - A. Recommend them immediately.
   - B. Wait for evidence about students' needs.
   - C. Remove all the existing tables.

5. Why will the notes have two sections?
   - A. To keep observation separate from opinion.
   - B. To compare two examination periods.
   - C. To list morning and afternoon classes.

6. Interview questions will be sent to the tutor on ______.

### Đáp án

1. **B** — Too brief được diễn đạt lại thành lack detail. (Đoạn 4: “replies might be too brief”)
2. **A** — Tuần thi có thể làm thay đổi thói quen học. (Đoạn 8: “a normal teaching week”)
3. **C** — Speaker B phụ trách buổi sáng và câu hỏi phỏng vấn. (Đoạn 11: “put our numbers into a spreadsheet”)
4. **B** — Đề xuất ban đầu được hoãn để đợi bằng chứng. (Đoạn 14: “wait until we know”)
5. **A** — Hai phần phản ánh hai loại thông tin, không phải thời gian. (Đoạn 16: “separate our observations from our personal opinions”)
6. **Friday** — Friday là hạn gửi câu hỏi; Monday là ngày bắt đầu thu thập. (Đoạn 18: “this Friday”)

## IELTS 6.0 — Bài giảng về đánh giá trải nghiệm bảo tàng

Part 4 · Note completion

Trích đoạn Part 4. Hoàn thành 6 ghi chú, viết ONE WORD ONLY cho mỗi câu. Nghiên cứu trong bài là tình huống giả định phục vụ luyện nghe. Nhãn 6.0 chưa được hiệu chuẩn bằng đề thi hoặc người học.

### Transcript

1. **A** (0.00–4.48s): Today we will examine how a museum can evaluate a new visitor route.
2. **A** (4.68–10.28s): Consider a hypothetical study in which a museum wants visitors to explore its smaller galleries.
3. **A** (10.48–16.32s): The initial proposal is to collect questionnaires at the exit, but this approach has a limitation.
4. **A** (16.52–21.40s): Visitors may remember their favourite exhibit without accurately recalling the route they followed.
5. **A** (21.60–26.56s): The team therefore combines the questionnaires with observations of movement through the building.
6. **A** (26.76–33.48s): Before introducing any changes, researchers record the existing patterns during one weekday and one weekend.
7. **A** (33.68–39.44s): This provides a baseline, although differences in attendance must be considered when the results are compared.
8. **A** (39.64–43.72s): A second issue concerns the people who agree to complete the questionnaires.
9. **A** (43.92–49.52s): If all the participants are volunteers, they may be more interested in the museum than other visitors.
10. **A** (49.72–55.96s): Consequently, their responses should not automatically be treated as representative of everyone who entered.
11. **A** (56.16–61.44s): Researchers also need to distinguish between the length of a visit and the visitor's satisfaction.
12. **A** (61.64–68.04s): Someone might remain in a gallery because the directions are confusing, rather than because the display is engaging.
13. **A** (68.24–73.52s): For the pilot, the team chooses to improve the signs instead of employing additional guides.
14. **A** (73.72–79.56s): The signs are cheaper to change, and their effect can be examined before a larger investment is made.
15. **A** (79.76–85.44s): Researchers then repeat the observations, keeping the opening hours and ticket prices unchanged.
16. **A** (85.64–92.04s): Nevertheless, an increase in visits to the smaller galleries would not by itself prove that the signs caused it.
17. **A** (92.24–99.04s): A temporary exhibition, for instance, might attract a different group of visitors during the second observation period.
18. **A** (99.24–106.60s): The final report should therefore acknowledge these uncertainties and recommend further testing, rather than promise a definite improvement.

### Câu hỏi

1. Questionnaires are combined with ______ of visitor movement.

2. Baseline data are collected on a weekday and a ______.

3. Using only ______ may produce an unrepresentative sample.

4. Visit length is not necessarily a measure of visitor ______.

5. The pilot improves the ______ rather than adding guides.

6. One possible outside influence is a temporary ______.

### Đáp án

1. **observations** — Bổ sung quan sát để khắc phục giới hạn trí nhớ của người trả lời. (Đoạn 5: “observations of movement”)
2. **weekend** — Hai loại ngày khác nhau giúp so sánh bối cảnh. (Đoạn 6: “one weekday and one weekend”)
3. **volunteers** — Người tự nguyện có thể quan tâm tới bảo tàng hơn nhóm còn lại. (Đoạn 9: “participants are volunteers”)
4. **satisfaction** — Ở lâu có thể vì chỉ dẫn khó hiểu, không phải vì hài lòng. (Đoạn 11: “visitor's satisfaction”)
5. **signs** — Guides là phương án bị thay thế trong thử nghiệm. (Đoạn 13: “improve the signs”)
6. **exhibition** — Triển lãm có thể làm thay đổi nhóm khách và gây nhiễu kết quả. (Đoạn 17: “A temporary exhibition”)

8. **A** (37.36–39.28s): Why must they change rooms?
9. **A** (47.28–49.04s): What will the woman do next?
10. **A** (57.04–61.20s): Hello, I'm calling about the paper we ordered for our print shop last Friday.
11. **B** (61.40–65.00s): I have the order here, and the delivery was scheduled for this morning.

## TOEIC 700 — Thông báo hội nghị và thay đổi dịch vụ

Part 4 · 2 talks × 3 questions

Đọc 6 câu hỏi và lựa chọn trước khi nghe. Mỗi bài nói có 3 câu hỏi, được đọc trong audio với khoảng nghỉ 8 giây. Mục tiêu 700 là tổng Listening & Reading tham khảo; bài nghe này không dự đoán tổng điểm.

### Transcript

1. **B** (0.00–4.88s): Before our afternoon conference sessions begin, I need to announce two changes to the programme.
2. **B** (5.08–9.96s): The workshop on customer retention will take place in room six, rather than the auditorium.
3. **B** (10.16–15.44s): Its presenter has requested a smaller setting so that participants can discuss their own experiences.
4. **B** (15.64–19.72s): The starting time remains two o'clock, and no new registration is required.
5. **B** (19.92–25.76s): The product demonstration has been postponed until three thirty because the display equipment has not arrived.
6. **B** (25.96–30.76s): If you booked transport for three, please speak to our reception team before lunch finishes.
7. **B** (30.96–36.00s): They may be able to adjust your departure, although a seat on the later shuttle cannot be guaranteed.
8. **A** (37.00–39.40s): Why has the workshop location changed?
9. **A** (47.40–50.20s): What caused the product demonstration to be delayed?
10. **A** (58.20–61.08s): What does the speaker suggest about the later shuttle?
11. **B** (69.08–73.80s): This is a message for tenants of the Riverside Business Centre about tomorrow's network upgrade.
12. **B** (74.00–80.16s): Internet access will be interrupted between seven and eight thirty in the morning while technicians replace the main equipment.
13. **B** (80.36–86.44s): Although most offices open at nine, anyone planning an earlier video call should arrange an alternative connection.
14. **B** (86.64–91.60s): The guest network will also be unavailable, so switching to that service will not solve the problem.
15. **B** (91.80–96.92s): Once the work is complete, you should restart your computer if it does not reconnect automatically.
16. **B** (97.12–103.20s): Please report any continuing difficulties to the service desk, rather than contacting your own internet provider.
17. **B** (103.40–107.40s): Our technicians will remain on site until midday to deal with those reports.
18. **A** (108.40–110.48s): Who is this message intended for?
19. **A** (118.48–121.28s): Why would using the guest network be ineffective?
20. **A** (129.28–133.04s): What should listeners do if a problem continues after the upgrade?

### Câu hỏi

1. 1. Why has the workshop location changed?
   - A. The auditorium needs repairs.
   - B. Fewer people registered than expected.
   - C. The presenter wants a more interactive setting.

2. 2. What caused the demonstration to be delayed?
   - A. An absent presenter.
   - B. Equipment that has not arrived.
   - C. A transport strike.
   - D. A registration error.

3. 3. What is suggested about the later shuttle?
   - A. It will be cancelled.
   - B. Every attendee has a reserved seat.
   - C. It leaves before the demonstration.

   - A. Businesses renting space in the centre.
   - B. Passengers at a railway station.
   - C. Guests checking into a hotel.
   - D. Students at a language school.

5. 5. Why would the guest network be ineffective?
   - A. Its password has changed.
   - B. Only visitors can use it.
   - C. It will be unavailable during the same work.
   - D. It cannot support any video calls.

6. 6. What should listeners do if a problem continues?
   - A. Contact their own internet provider.
   - B. Report it to the service desk.
   - C. Wait until the following week.

### Đáp án

1. **C** — Interactive setting diễn đạt lại mục đích trao đổi trải nghiệm. (Đoạn 3: “participants can discuss their own experiences”)
2. **B** — Thiếu thiết bị, không phải thay phòng workshop. (Đoạn 5: “display equipment has not arrived”)
3. **D** — Không bảo đảm chỗ là căn cứ suy ra sức chứa có thể hạn chế. (Đoạn 7: “a seat on the later shuttle cannot be guaranteed”)
4. **A** — Tenants ở business centre là các đơn vị thuê văn phòng. (Đoạn 11: “tenants of the Riverside Business Centre”)
5. **C** — Cả mạng khách cũng bị ngắt trong đợt nâng cấp. (Đoạn 14: “guest network will also be unavailable”)
6. **B** — Người nói chỉ rõ đầu mối hỗ trợ và bác bỏ internet provider. (Đoạn 16: “report any continuing difficulties to the service desk”)

## COMMUNICATION A1 — Gọi món ở quán cà phê

Hội thoại đời sống · A1

Nghe hai người gọi món. Có thể nghe lại ở chế độ luyện. Trả lời 4 câu để kiểm tra thông tin chính; sau đó mở transcript để chép từng câu. Mức A1 là thiết kế tham khảo theo CEFR.

### Transcript

1. **A** (0.00–2.72s): Hello, can I have a small tea, please?
2. **B** (3.52–6.08s): Yes, would you like milk in your tea?
3. **A** (6.88–10.32s): No milk, thank you, but I would like some sugar.
4. **B** (11.12–13.68s): The sugar is on the table near the window.
5. **A** (14.48–16.72s): Do you have any cheese sandwiches today?
